About C&B Consulting

Decisions built on solid ground.

C&B Consulting places deep due diligence at the centre of its advisory work, so the financial and strategic guidance we provide rests on verified facts. Based in London, we work closely with a select portfolio of clients to secure better decisions.

Clients bring us in at the moments that matter most: weighing an acquisition, taking on a new partner, committing capital or steering the business through a difficult stretch. In each case, the value is the same: knowing exactly where you stand before you act.

Our Approach

We hold ourselves to a high standard of evidence. A finding makes it into your report only after we've established it from primary sources and verified it ourselves, because we refuse to pass on what we can't stand behind.

That judgement comes from experience. Having examined countless businesses, our team knows exactly where problems tend to hide and which details deserve a closer look. Before the review even begins, we work to understand your industry and your unique market position. We then shape our scope around the specific decision in front of you, taking the investigation as deep as that choice warrants.

At C&B Consulting, you are guaranteed a measured account of our findings. We deliver the key considerations alongside recommended next steps, all written in plain language. From the outset, you'll know exactly who is leading your engagement and how to reach them, ensuring every stage is purpose-built to move your decision forward.

Our Capabilities

Due Diligence

This is the heart of what we do. Before you acquire, invest, partner or sign, we confirm the opportunity is sound and surface anything that should give you pause. We trace ownership and corporate structure, scrutinise financial records and trading history, and assess the market reputation.

Some clients use our findings to proceed with confidence. Others use them to renegotiate or to walk away before a costly mistake. Each of those is a result worth having.

Financial Review · Background Checks · Corporate Structure · Reputational Risk

Financial Solutions

We advise on the financial questions behind transactions, business planning, tax, compliance, disputes and risk management. Our consultants get close to how your business operates, so the guidance fits your circumstances. Whether you're absorbing a slowdown or funding a period of growth, the aim holds steady: financial decisions you can trust completely.

Transaction support · Risk management · Tax & compliance · Capital planning

Business Strategy

A great strategy starts with an honest look at where the business stands and where the market is heading. We help clients review direction, test assumptions, set priorities and strengthen the way the business is organised. Our work includes strategic review, market assessment, planning support and progress measures. We also establish clear measures so you can track progress as you go.

Market Assessment · Strategic Planning · Growth Planning · Performance Measures
